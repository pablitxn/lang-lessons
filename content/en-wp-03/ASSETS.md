# Image-cue and asset strategy

All 25 `image_cue` records are original authored briefs, with `file: null` and
`asset_status: brief-only`. No image is generated, downloaded, licensed or
reviewed in this package. Briefs identify an action or sense rather than a
tense. Abstract be, become, begin, cost, come and bring need comprehension
testing: a still image cannot prove lexical uniqueness or reference direction.
Keep a visible-base fallback available when the cue is ambiguous.

Preferred next step: commission simple original illustrations with explicit
usage rights. Alternatively use verified public-domain/CC0 assets, or compatible
attribution-licensed assets after checking the specific item's rights and the
app's distribution needs. Search-result thumbnails are not a license. No paid
commission, provider call, external download or publication is authorized here.
Do not copy dictionary illustrations or copyrighted characters. Use consistent
contrast, clear silhouettes and neutral settings without brand marks, verb
labels, morphology or time/tense labels. Blood is a tiny non-graphic cue; bet
uses playful adult predictions without real-money promotion.

Before shipping an actual asset, the asset owner records this proposed ledger:

| Field | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| content_id / asset_id | Stable verb reference and unique asset ID |
| origin / creator | Original creator or exact source item URL and capture date |
| license / license_url | Specific license/version or written ownership permission |
| attribution | Exact required credit and how the app supplies it |
| modifications | Crop, adaptation and derivative permission, if applicable |
| artifact_path / sha256 | Local file and immutable checksum |
| review | Rights reviewer/date, comprehension review, mobile rendering and accessibility checks |
| restrictions | Required releases, distribution limits or unresolved restrictions |

No invented ledger entry certifies rights. Keep attribution separable from the
exercise and avoid personal/identifiable subjects without appropriate releases.
Unresolved rights block shipping that asset, not the authored curriculum.

`alt` is a descriptive accessibility draft, not a secure hidden-verb prompt. An
honest description can reveal the verb. EN-WP-02/01 must define an equivalent
accessible exercise, such as opting into visible-base mode, with transparent
scoring. Do not hide useful alt text or silently count a revealed identity as
successful image inference. Cue captions, alt text and helper tables must not
leak unrelated answer forms or erase stage/evidence. In-call helpers are a
separate surface from the opt-in genuine-error phonetic help.
